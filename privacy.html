clip-studio privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

clip-studio is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload short videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What data it uses

With your permission through Google sign-in, clip-studio uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to your channel and to read your channel's name. It does not read, collect or store any other personal data.

Where data is stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than YouTube's.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with anyone.

Removing access

You can remove clip-studio's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

clip-studio's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
